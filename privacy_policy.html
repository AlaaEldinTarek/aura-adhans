Privacy Policy — Aura (هالة)

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Aura (هالة) is a free Islamic prayer and productivity app developed by Alaa Eldin Tarek. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location: Your device location is used to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is processed on-device and is never stored on our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Account Information: If you sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In, we store your name and email address in Firebase to sync your tasks and prayer records across devices.
  • Prayer & Task Data: Your prayer tracking records, tasks, and app preferences are stored in Firebase Firestore to enable cloud sync. This data is private to your account.
  • Usage Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (e.g., which features are used) to improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your location
  • To sync your tasks, prayer records, and preferences across devices
  • To send prayer time notifications and reminders
  • To improve app features using anonymous analytics

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your data is only shared with the following services that power the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy

4. Guest Mode

You can use Aura without creating an account (guest mode). In guest mode, all data is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to any server.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Profile screen.

6. Children's Privacy

Aura is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Permissions

  • Location: For prayer times and Qibla compass
  • Notifications: For prayer time alerts and reminders
  • Do Not Disturb / Ringer Mode: For automatic silent mode during prayers (user-configurable)
  • Display Over Other Apps: For Focus Mode overlay and full-screen Adhan
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: To ensure prayer alarms fire reliably in the background
